# Adding photos to your website

No code, no app to install. Just a web page.

## Logging in

1. Go to **yoursite.netlify.app/admin** (Akash will give you your actual site address).
2. First time only: check your email for an invite, click the link, and set a password.
3. After that, just go to that `/admin` address any time and log in with your email and password.

## Adding a new photo

1. Once logged in, click **Photo Gallery** on the left, then **Photos**.
2. You'll see the list of every photo currently on the site. Scroll to the bottom and click **Add Photos** (or the **+** button, depending on your screen).
3. Fill in:
   - **Photo**: click to upload the image from your computer or phone.
   - **Title**: a short name for it, shown when someone opens the photo full-size. A few words is plenty, like "West Pier, Sunset."
   - **Description**: a plain sentence describing what's in the photo. This helps people using screen readers, and helps the photo get found in search engines. Just describe what you see.
   - **Categories**: tick every category that fits, Street, Coast, Portraits, Black & White, Design. These control the filter buttons above the gallery, so a photo can be in more than one.
   - **Display this extra wide**: turn this on if you want the photo to stand out, it'll take up double the width in the grid. Works best on wide, landscape photos. Leave it off for most photos.
   - **This is a black & white photo**: turn on for black & white shots.
4. Click **Publish** at the top right (it may say "Publish now" or similar).
5. Give it a minute or two, then refresh the actual website, your photo will be there.

## Editing or removing a photo

1. Same place, **Photo Gallery → Photos**.
2. Click on any photo in the list to open and edit it, change the title, swap the image, adjust categories, whatever's needed.
3. To remove one, open it and look for a small trash or delete icon, usually near the top of that photo's box.
4. Click **Publish** again after any change, that's what makes it go live.

## Reordering photos

Each photo in the list has a small drag handle (usually dots or lines on the left side). Click and hold it, then drag the photo up or down to change where it appears on the site. Don't forget to **Publish** after.

## A few things worth knowing

- Nothing goes live until you click **Publish**. You can safely open something to look at it and just navigate away if you change your mind.
- Changes take a minute or two to appear on the actual site after publishing, that's normal, it's rebuilding in the background.
- Photos display best around 1600 to 2000 pixels wide. Anything from a modern phone camera is already more than enough, no need to resize anything yourself.
- If you're ever stuck or something looks wrong, it's hard to break anything permanently, everything is saved with history, so mistakes are easy to undo. Just ask Akash if you're unsure.
